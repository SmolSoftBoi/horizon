# Smelly A.F. Shopify theme

A Smelly A.F. storefront built on this checkout of Shopify Horizon. The theme uses the linked Branding Brainstorm’s cream and candy-pink direction, bold serif headings, handwritten notes, original candle photographs and four illustrated A.F. ranges.

## Included

- Editable homepage sections: hero, candle details, best sellers, ranges, story, journal and questions.
- “Create a cosier, happier home” uses the existing `smelly-story` section: a blush background, original candle photograph in a cream frame, large serif heading, shopping button and handwritten heart signature. Its copy, caption, image, image description and button destination are editable; new sections appear as **Smelly A.F. cosier home** in the section picker. Blank fields disappear, and long captions stay within the frame.
- Best sellers retains the existing `smelly-featured` section and its saved photograph blocks. Its responsive grid shows up to eight products, with live prices and native basket controls, or the original editorial photographs without sample prices. Names, notes, introduction, photographs and editorial scent links remain editable.
- All scents (`smelly-all-scents`) supplies the default collection template, so existing Shop all scents links open the branded catalogue. It shows real products in Shopify's collection order, with prices, basket controls, sorting, configured storefront filters and numbered pagination. Range links, introduction and products per page are editable. Other collections retain their own title and description. Filtering, sorting, range navigation and pagination use native forms and links, including with JavaScript disabled.
- Illustrated candle details with pouring-jug, soy-sprig and jar options, serif headings and fine separators. The strip uses equal columns on desktop, centred illustrations on tablet and stacked rows on mobile. Original icon choices remain available; blank entries stay selectable in the editor and are hidden from shoppers.
- Eight shared vector illustrations use rounded ink outlines. The four range motifs combine opaque colour, finer interior lines and cream highlights: an orange pumpkin with a vine and crescent, a pink ribbon-tied candy cane with green holly, a pleated sweet with a bold pink swirl, and a cream daisy with a golden centre and veined leaves. Opaque layers keep the cane’s stripes behind the ribbon. Small detail icons and heart accents retain their inherited tints and heavier strokes. Wax pours into an open jar with an exposed wick; the closed jar stays unlit.
- Illustrated range cards use generous arched colour panels, cream artwork backdrops and a divided explore link. The whole card opens its selected collection. Four ranges sit in one row from 990px and two columns below it; fewer ranges stay centred. Custom artwork and existing range settings remain editable. Empty sections stay hidden from shoppers while retaining their introduction in the editor.
- Compact, centred header wordmark, pink navigation and basket accents, and spaced mobile controls. An uploaded logo takes precedence over the text wordmark. The newsletter footer uses the same branding.
- A full-width lifestyle hero with an editable headline and shopping button over the photograph. Desktop, tablet and mobile compositions keep the candles clear of the text. The hero appears immediately; button arrows, product-photo zoom, range illustrations and FAQ answers follow the visitor’s reduced-motion preference.
- About (`page.about`) and scent guide (`page.scents`) templates.
- A branded default product template with a large media gallery, serif product title, live price, product description, quantity selector and pink Add to basket control. It retains Horizon’s variant picker, gallery thumbnails, image zoom, accelerated checkout, sticky basket bar, product recommendations, disclosures and analytics. The handwritten photograph caption and care information are editable; shipping and refund links use the store’s configured policies and stay hidden when unavailable. Products without media use Horizon’s centred details layout.
- Horizon’s existing search, basket, contact, journal and article templates, with the shared brand palette and typography. Its original collection section remains available in the theme source.
- Shopify product forms for featured products, including sold-out states. Products with variants or selling plans link to the product page for selection. Direct adds start at the variant’s minimum order quantity, round the resulting basket quantity to its required increment, and respect maximums and managed stock. Each submission takes shoppers to the basket.
  Shopify requires quantity-rule minima and maxima to be multiples of the increment, as enforced by [quantity rule validation](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/2026-10/mutations/quantityRulesAdd). The direct-add calculation follows that contract.
- A Shopify customer newsletter form with error and success messages, an explicit marketing notice and the store’s privacy-policy link when configured.

## Store setup

1. Upload the supplied theme ZIP as a new, unpublished theme in **Online Store → Themes**. Keep the live theme until the preview has been checked.
2. In the theme editor, open **Smelly A.F. best sellers** and select its collection. Set that collection’s product order to **Best selling** in Shopify admin for sales-based ordering, or use a manually curated order. The section preserves the collection’s order and does not calculate sales rankings. Without a populated selected collection, it displays the supplied photographs as an editorial gallery, without invented prices or purchasing controls; these photographs do not establish sales rankings. Optional scent links can point each editorial card to its product page. With no products or photograph blocks, the section stays hidden from shoppers and remains visible in the editor.
3. Connect **Spooky A.F.**, **Festive A.F.**, **Sweet A.F.** and **Fresh A.F.** to the appropriate collections. Unassigned range cards link to all products.
   In the default collection template, connect the matching range links in **Smelly A.F. all scents**. Unassigned catalogue links stay hidden from shoppers and appear disabled in the editor. Configure storefront filters in Shopify Search & Discovery; the section uses those filters without inferring scent ranges from product names or tags. Shoppers choose filters and a sort order, then select **Apply**. Empty collections show an editable message; filtered views offer clear-all and individual removal links. The catalogue uses store products only and does not substitute editorial photographs or sample prices.
4. Create or select the About and Scents pages and assign their matching templates. In **Smelly A.F. story**, the homepage button opens all scents by default. Change **Button link** to choose another destination, or clear it and select **Story page** to link to About. Clear both destinations or the button label to hide the button. Page titles and page content come from Shopify.
5. Configure the header’s existing `main-menu` with Shop, Scents, About, Journal and Contact links to the actual store resources. Select the footer menu and contact page. The theme does not create or change navigation, products, collections, pages, blog articles or policies.
6. Select a blog in the homepage journal section. That section stays hidden from shoppers until the selected blog has articles.
7. Review editable copy, set the shop’s name, upload a final logo/favicon if available, and configure the store’s policies and payment methods. Check email marketing settings and any confirmation flow required by the store.
8. Preview a real product with variants, an unavailable product, search, Add to basket, basket quantity changes, contact submission and newsletter signup on desktop and mobile before publishing.

The default product template enables **Use Smelly A.F. product styling** in **Smelly A.F. product**. The setting is off by default for other instances of Horizon’s Product information section. Product titles, prices, descriptions, media and availability come from the selected store product. Edit each product’s description in Shopify to supply its scent details. Edit or clear **Photograph caption** in the section settings, and use **Smelly A.F. care & info** inside Product details to edit the care heading/text or disable policy links. Clearing the care heading or text hides that row; clearing the policy heading hides the policy row. The care and policy rows use native disclosures and remain usable without JavaScript. Gallery settings and purchasing behaviour remain Horizon’s own; test variant changes, quantity rules, stock errors, image zoom, accelerated checkout and the sticky basket bar with real store data.

## Brand controls and content

`Smelly A.F. branding` in Theme settings enables the text wordmark and classic serif headings. The header’s solid and transparent states and the footer honour this setting; uploaded logos take precedence, and disabling branding falls back to the store name. Horizon’s existing colour palette, button and spacing controls remain available. Brand sections use the shared `assets/smelly-af.css` palette; their text, images and resource links are editable in the theme editor.

The five original product photographs show Pink Bubblegum, Pumpkin Spice, Candy Cane, Parma Violets and Christmas Spice. They remain exact copies of the supplied files, with their labels, wax colours, lids and jar proportions unchanged. Every photographed candle is unlit with its lid on.

## Hero imagery

The three `assets/smelly-hero-lifestyle-*.jpg` files are generated lifestyle composites made with the built-in image generator using the supplied product photographs as identity references. Desktop shows all four hero scents; tablet and mobile focus on Pink Bubblegum and Pumpkin Spice. Visual checks compare jar proportions, gold lids, wax colours, label artwork, scent names, 150g and EST. 2026 with the originals. All candles remain unlit with their lids on.

- `smelly-hero-lifestyle-desktop.jpg`: 2172 × 724, used from 990px.
- `smelly-hero-lifestyle-tablet.jpg`: 1536 × 1024, used at 750–989px.
- `smelly-hero-lifestyle-mobile.jpg`: 1024 × 1536, used below 750px.

The theme uses a native `picture` element and proportionate image scaling. The headline, description and shopping button are live HTML over the scene. Optional notes and the handwritten annotation remain editable. Desktop, tablet and mobile image pickers support their own focal points; an unselected smaller-screen image inherits a selected larger-screen photograph. With no uploads selected, the matching bundled scenes apply. Edit the shared image description when replacing the photography: it takes priority for every size. Leaving it blank uses the desktop image’s alt text, then the shop name.

When the hero is the first section under a transparent header, it reserves the measured header space for its text and annotation. The photograph retains its proportions and blends into that extra space. RTL writing keeps its direction while the text block stays in the clear area on the left. A hero further down the page receives no extra header offset.

Hand-poured soy wax and 150g are supported by the supplied labels. No mock prices, reviews, discounts, testimonials, burn times, delivery promises or vegan/plastic-free claims have been added. Replace the proposed brand copy where necessary before launch.

New interface strings are in the `smelly` namespace in storefront locale files. The shared `shop_all` navigation label is translated in all 34 storefront locales. Other Smelly A.F. strings use UK English as an explicit fallback in the inherited languages; translate those remaining strings and editable content before enabling another storefront language. Existing Horizon translations are preserved.

Theme Check passes with **0 errors and 6 warnings** using the installed Shopify CLI 4.9.4 and current Shopify Liquid definitions. The warnings concern the header’s setting count and five unused LiquidDoc parameters in the existing divider snippet.

The previously reported 141 errors were false positives from a stale local definition cache, not invalid Horizon code. The cached files dated from February 2026 and did not recognise colour placeholders, the global colour palette or the two newer Liquid filters. On 20 October 2026, the cache was backed up and refreshed through Theme Check’s built-in downloader to Shopify definition revision `40f966fc7793462726d9f8573c8d522f8ab44c54`. The same standard checks then passed. No checks were disabled and no theme features were removed.

Shopify documents these features in [input settings](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/architecture/settings/input-settings), [standard event data](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/liquid/filters/standard_event_data) and [applicable disclosures](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/liquid/filters/applicable_disclosures). If these false positives recur on another machine, check that Theme Check can refresh its local Shopify definitions before changing valid theme code.

A local visual preview cannot verify Shopify’s server rendering, live catalogue, checkout, newsletter subscription or store configuration. Those checks require an authenticated store preview.
